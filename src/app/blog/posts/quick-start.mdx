## About

There was a time when coffee was simply part of my routine—something I drank to wake up, to focus, or just to get through the day.

I enjoyed coffee, but I never really stopped to think about it.

Not until I had my first cup of specialty coffee.

Something about that experience felt different.  
The flavors were unfamiliar.  
The acidity surprised me.  
The aroma stayed with me longer than I expected.

For the first time, coffee felt like something to be explored, not just consumed.

That curiosity slowly became a habit.

I started learning more about coffee origins, roast profiles, and brewing methods.  
I bought my first brewing tool—a moka pot—and began experimenting at home, one cup at a time.

Some brews turned out better than others.  
Some were too bitter.  
Some were unexpectedly memorable.

But every cup taught me something.

## The Meaning Behind derd.time

The name **derd** comes from something deeply personal.

It’s a combination of two names:  
**Dimas Erlangga** and **Roselina Diyan**.

A small way of bringing both of us into this space.

And the word **time** carries its own meaning.

It represents the time we share—  
the quiet moments, the slow mornings, and the little rituals that often matter the most.

In a world that moves too fast, coffee has become one of the reasons to pause.

To sit down.  
To slow down.  
To be present.

To make time for what matters.

That’s what **derd.time** really means to me:

**our time.**

---

## A Space to Document the Journey

This space was created to document my journey into specialty coffee.

Not as an expert.  
Not as a professional barista.

Just as someone who is learning—slowly, curiously, and enjoying every step of the process.

Here, I’ll be sharing:

- Coffee beans I’ve tried  
- Brewing experiments and notes  
- Favorite tools and setups  
- Things I’m learning about specialty coffee  
- Small moments worth remembering  

Some entries may be simple.  
Some may be more reflective.

But each one is part of the same journey.

---

## Just Getting Started

I’m still learning.

My palate is still developing.  
My brewing technique is still improving.  
There’s still so much I don’t know.

And maybe that’s what makes it exciting.

**derd.time** is not about perfection.

It’s about curiosity.  
Consistency.  
And appreciating the process—one cup at a time.

Thank you for being here.

I hope this space can become a small reminder to slow down, enjoy the ritual, and make time—for coffee, for reflection, and for the people we share it with.
